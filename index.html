How spaced repetition works

The SM-2 algorithm schedules each question at the optimal moment — just before you would forget it. Answer correctly and the interval grows: 1 day → 6 days → weeks → months. Answer incorrectly and it resets.

Each session mixes due reviews (questions whose interval has expired) and new questions (up to 20 total). Aim to clear your due queue daily — 15 minutes a day beats 3 hours the night before.
